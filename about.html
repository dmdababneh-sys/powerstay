Powerstay is a specialized hotel investment and management firm. We acquire, manage, and operate hospitality assets with a focus on real results and clear communication.

Our Foundation

A New Standard in
Hotel Asset Management.

Powerstay was founded to bring real discipline and structure to hotel operations. We observed a market filled with uncertainty and inefficiency, and built a model that guarantees stability for property owners.

We prioritize actionable results over empty promises, leveraging a highly organized corporate structure to maximize the value of every asset in our portfolio.

Expert Hotel Management

Our seasoned General Managers and operational directors come from top-tier hospitality backgrounds. They enforce rigorous SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures), ensuring 5-star cleanliness, exceptional guest satisfaction, and seamless daily workflows.

Revenue Managers (RevPRO)

Our specialized Revenue Management unit constantly analyzes market trends, competitor pricing, and demand forecasts. Using dynamic pricing algorithms, we maximize Average Daily Rate (ADR) and RevPAR to ensure the asset yields the highest possible return.

Dedicated Maintenance & Engineering

Protecting the physical asset is our priority. We deploy a rapid-response maintenance and engineering team capable of preventative maintenance, CapEx planning, and immediate troubleshooting, ensuring the property's lifecycle is extended and value is preserved.
