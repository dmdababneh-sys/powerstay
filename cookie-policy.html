Cookie Policy

Information about how we use cookies and tracking technologies.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of text sent by your web browser by a website you visit. A cookie file is stored in your web browser and allows the Service or a third-party to recognize you and make your next visit easier and the Service more useful to you.

2. How Powerstay uses Cookies

When you use and access the Website, we may place a number of cookies files in your web browser. We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • To enable certain functions of the Website.
  • To provide analytics.
  • To store your preferences.

3. Third-party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, we may also use various third-parties cookies to report usage statistics of the Website and deliver advertisements on and through the Website.

4. What are your choices regarding cookies

If you'd like to delete cookies or instruct your web browser to delete or refuse cookies, please visit the help pages of your web browser. Please note, however, that if you delete cookies or refuse to accept them, you might not be able to use all of the features we offer, you may not be able to store your preferences, and some of our pages might not display properly.
